HOW THE ACO PART WORKS

Ants construct complete train plans

Each ant selects a route for every train using track pheromone and a travel-time heuristic. It then assigns an available destination platform and a crew team qualified for that service. Short, feasible plans receive more pheromone, making their choices more likely in later iterations.

This is a coursework simulator with synthetic train schedules and illustrative travel times. It does not represent a live railway or safety-critical dispatch system.
